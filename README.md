### Hi!
**My name's John Woods, but everyone calls me Jack.** I study Electrical Engineering at the University of Michigan. Classes are important, but I've always thought the best learning happens outside the lecture hall. I co-founded [Wave RF](https://wave-rf.com), where we build highly reliable communication and networking systems. My work there has never had clearly defined boundaries; I've designed RF PCBs, negotiated VC investment deals, run payroll, swept the floors, and a thousand other things.

Previously, I worked as a lab instructor for two first-year engineering classes focused on rocket science and weather balloons, and as Drivetrain Director for MRacing, Michigan's Formula Student team.

When I'm not at work or deep in a side project, you can probably find me working on my home server, reading about the intersections of tech, policy, and economics, or watching engineering-focused YouTube videos.

### What I Work On
**Hardware** — PCB design (KiCad and Altium Designer), CAD (Fusion360, NX, Onshape), RF/wireless, power systems, test electronics

**Firmware** — C/C++, PlatformIO, Arduino, ESP32, nRF, ATmega

**Software** — C/C++, Python, MATLAB, Docker, Linux, git

**Interests** — amateur radio, telecommunications, aerospace, self-hosted infrastructure, embedded systems

**A philosophical side note:** I don't like labels. I'm not an *electrical* engineer or a *mechanical* engineer — I'm an *engineer*. To me, that means I solve problems with logic and critical thinking; the electrical expertise, mechanical expertise, and software expertise are all just tools in the toolbox used in pursuit of that goal: **solve problems.**
